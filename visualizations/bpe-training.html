Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) Training

Watch how BPE learns a subword vocabulary by repeatedly merging the most frequent adjacent pair of tokens.

What is BPE?

Byte-Pair Encoding starts from a small base vocabulary — either individual characters or raw UTF-8 bytes — and iteratively merges the most frequent adjacent pair into a new token. Each merge grows the vocabulary by one entry. After many merges, common substrings (“the”, “ing”, even whole words) become single tokens. This is how modern tokenizers (GPT-2, GPT-3, LLaMA, etc.) build their vocabularies before training a language model.
