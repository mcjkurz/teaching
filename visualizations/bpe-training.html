Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) Training

Watch BPE learn a subword vocabulary by repeatedly merging the most frequent pair of adjacent tokens.

What is BPE?

Byte-Pair Encoding starts from a small base vocabulary — individual characters or raw UTF-8 bytes — and repeatedly merges the most frequent adjacent pair into a new token. Each merge grows the vocabulary by one. After many merges, common substrings (“the”, “ing”, whole words) become single tokens. This is how modern tokenizers (GPT-2, LLaMA, etc.) build their vocabularies.

Pre-tokenization splits text on spaces first, so merges never cross word boundaries (toggle it below to compare). Base tokens show their content (a character or byte); each merged token is shown as its new vocabulary ID (e.g. 256 (th)). Press Next Merge to step through the algorithm.
