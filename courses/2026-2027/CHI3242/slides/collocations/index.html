什麼是搭配詞？What is a collocation?

兩個詞傾向於在自然語言中彼此靠近出現，稱為「共現」（cooccur）。 A combination of two words that tend to occur near each other in natural language, i.e. to cooccur.
Evert, “Corpora and collocations”, p. 1214
有些詞互相「吸引」Some words attract each other
cow – milkday – nightring – bell喝 – 茶天氣 – 好
有些則不然Others do not
know – glassdoor – year喝 – 桌子
問題：如何「測量」這種吸引力，並與偶然區分開？ How do we measure attraction, and tell it apart from chance?

隨機的詞 vs. 真實的語言Random words vs. real language

虛無假說：詞語是隨機分布的Null hypothesis: words are distributed randomly
自然語言：詞語的分布是有意義的Natural language: words are distributed meaningfully
搭配詞 ＝ 相鄰出現的頻率高於隨機打亂所預期的詞對 A collocation is a pair that sits together more often than random shuffling would predict.

期望值 EExpected frequency: what would we see if it were all chance?

10 顆球，其中 5 顆紅球：P(紅) ＝ 5 / 10 ＝ 0.510 balls, 5 of them red

再取對數，就得到互信息 MI（下一頁）。Taking the logarithm of this ratio gives mutual information (next slide).

互信息 MIMutual information (pointwise): the log of O / E

對 O/E 取以 2 為底的對數Take the base-2 logarithm of O / E
MI = log2( O / E )
王婆 ／ 痛苦：for our example pair
MI = log2(8 / 1.2) = log2 6.7 ≈ bits
來源：Evert, p. 1226。MI 只看效應大小（O 與 E 的比值），不考慮證據多寡——低頻詞對容易得高分（p. 1227）。Evert, p. 1226. MI only measures effect size, not how much evidence we have; rare pairs can score very high (p. 1227).
為什麼取對數？Why the logarithm?

PPMI：正的點互信息PPMI: positive pointwise mutual information

負的 MI 表示「彼此排斥」：比偶然更少共現Negative MI means repulsion: the words co-occur less than chance
PPMI：把負值一律設為 0PPMI: replace every negative value with 0
PPMI = max( 0, log2( O / E ) )
為什麼？要可靠地判斷「比偶然更少」，需要很大的語料；低頻詞對的負值大多只是噪音。PPMI 因此常用於詞向量／共現矩陣（分布式語義）。Reliably detecting “less than chance” needs a huge corpus; for rare pairs negative values are mostly noise. PPMI is therefore widely used for co-occurrence matrices and word vectors (distributional semantics).
例：the Iliad 與 must also 的 O ＝ E，所以 MI ＝ PPMI ＝ 0：沒有超出偶然的吸引力。Example: for the Iliad and must also, O = E, so MI = PPMI = 0: no attraction beyond chance.

預備知識：n 選 kBackground: “n choose k”

從 n 個東西中，選出 k 個（不計順序），有幾種選法？In how many ways can we pick k items out of n, when order does not matter?
＝ n!k! (n − k)!
n! ＝ n × (n−1) × … × 2 × 1（階乘）。例：4 選 2：n! (factorial) = n × (n−1) × … × 1. Example: 4 choose 2:
＝ 4!2! · 2!＝ 242 · 2＝ 6

奶茶實驗：8 杯選 4 杯「先牛奶」，共有 C(8,4) ＝ 70 種可能；只有 1 種全對 → p ＝ 1/70。Tea experiment: choosing which 4 of 8 cups are milk-first gives C(8,4) = 70 possibilities; only 1 is fully correct, so p = 1/70.
