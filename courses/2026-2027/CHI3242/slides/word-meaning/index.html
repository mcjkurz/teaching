語義是什麼？語言中只有差異What is word meaning? Language is a system of differences

「語言中只有差異，沒有正面成分。」“In language there are only differences without positive terms.” — Ferdinand de Saussure

四個詞都和「高興」有關，但各有各的位置：一個詞的意義，來自它和其他詞的區別。 Four words around “happy”, each with its own place: meaning comes from how a word differs from the others.

三種看法：差異、使用、搭配Three views: difference, use, company

Saussure1857–1913
語言中只有差異，沒有正面成分。“In language there are only differences without positive terms.”
Wittgenstein1889–1951
一個詞的意義在於它在語言中的使用。“The meaning of a word is its use in the language.” Philosophical Investigations §43
Firth1890–1960
觀其伴，知其義。“You shall know a word by the company it keeps.” A Synopsis of Linguistic Theory (1957)
共同的方向：不要問「這個詞指什麼」，而要看它怎麼被使用、和誰一起出現。 The common thread: do not ask what a word “stands for”; look at how it is used and what it occurs with.

Harris：用「環境」定義意義Harris: meaning as distribution

如果 A 和 B 有幾乎相同的環境，我們說它們是同義詞。如果 A 和 B 有一些共同的環境，也有一些不同，我們就說它們有不同的含義——意義差異的多少，大致相當於環境差異的多少。 “If A and B have almost identical environments … we say they are synonyms … If A and B have some environments in common and some not … we say that they have different meanings, the amount of meaning difference corresponding roughly to the amount of difference in their environments.” Distributional Structure (1954)

目標：讓機器算出詞與詞的相似度Goal: let a machine compute how similar two words are

similarity(“香蕉”, “網絡”)= −0.3

這需要把每個詞變成數字，而且是可以比較、可以計算的數字：這就是向量。 For this, every word must become numbers that can be compared and computed with: a vector.

向量 (vector)Vectors

向量：具有大小和方向的量，可以畫成帶箭頭的線段。A vector has magnitude and direction; draw it as an arrow.
a = [3, 2]
b = [−2, 3]
[3, 2]：向右 3 格，向上 2 格。[3, 2]: three steps right, two steps up.
標量 (scalar) 只有大小，如 3；向量還有方向。A scalar has only magnitude, like 3.

向量可以有很多維度Vectors can have many dimensions

a = [3, 2, 1]
b = [−4, 2, 2]
三維還畫得出來。十維呢？Three dimensions can still be drawn. Ten?
a = [1, 5.2, 4, 2.4, −5.1, 7, 1.5, 2.4, −4, 2.1]
b = [−4, 2, 2, 3, −2.4, 5.4, 3, −4.1, 2.5, 0.1]
畫不出來，但運算規則完全一樣。詞向量通常有幾十到幾百個維度。We cannot draw it, but the arithmetic is the same. Word vectors typically have tens to hundreds of dimensions.

內積 (dot product)The dot product

\(\vec a\cdot\vec b=\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i b_i = a_1b_1+a_2b_2+\cdots+a_nb_n\)
對應位置相乘，再全部相加。結果是一個數字。Multiply position by position, then add everything up. The result is a single number.
兩個向量在同一個位置上「同號」，這一項就為正；「異號」就為負。內積越大，兩者越「一致」。Same signs at a position add to the score; opposite signs subtract. The larger the dot product, the more the vectors agree.

先猜：哪個內積更大？（別算！）Guess first: which dot product is larger? (Don’t calculate!)

提示：只看每個位置是「同號」還是「異號」。Hint: look only at whether the signs match.
a · b = 41 ＞ c · d = −48。a 與 b 每個位置都同號；c 與 d 每個位置都異號。a · b = 41 ＞ c · d = −48: a and b agree everywhere; c and d disagree everywhere.

記法：a · b 就是 aTbNotation: the dot product as a matrix product

在數學和程式裡，向量預設是一欄（column）：By convention, a vector is a column.
\(\mathbf a=\begin{pmatrix}3\\2\\0.5\end{pmatrix}\quad \mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\3.5\\2\end{pmatrix}\)
轉置 (transpose) T：把一欄翻成一列。Transpose ᵀ turns the column into a row.
\(\mathbf a^{\mathsf T}=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 2 & 0.5\end{pmatrix}\)
(1 × 3)·(3 × 1)→(1 × 1)一個數字 a single number
矩陣乘法：一列 × 一欄，正好就是內積Matrix multiplication: row times column = multiply pairs and add.
\(\mathbf a^{\mathsf T}\mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 2 & 0.5\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}-1\\3.5\\2\end{pmatrix}\)
\(=3(-1)+2(3.5)+0.5(2)=\mathbf{5}\)
為什麼這樣寫？因為它把內積放進矩陣運算：一次乘法就能算出成千上萬個內積（例如所有詞與「思」的分數）。程式裡寫作 a @ b 或 np.dot(a, b)。Why? It turns dot products into matrix algebra: one matrix product computes thousands of them at once (e.g. every word against 思). In code: a @ b, np.dot(a, b).

用夾角衡量相似Measuring similarity with the angle

\(\text{similarity}(\vec a,\vec b)=\cos\theta=\dfrac{\vec a\cdot\vec b}{\lVert\vec a\rVert\,\lVert\vec b\rVert}\)

餘弦函數：夾角 → 相似度The cosine function: from angle to similarity

夾角只在 0°–180° 之間：這一段 cos 從 +1 一路降到 −1，夾角越大，越不相似。Angles lie in 0°–180°, where cos falls steadily from +1 to −1: wider angle, lower similarity.

為什麼要除以長度？Why divide by the lengths?

內積會被向量的長度左右：高頻詞的向量往往更長。The dot product is swayed by vector length; frequent words tend to have longer vectors.
\(\vec v=[6,8]\)，\(\lVert\vec v\rVert=\sqrt{36+64}=10\)
\(\vec u=\dfrac{\vec v}{\lVert\vec v\rVert}=[0.6,\,0.8]\)，\(\lVert\vec u\rVert=1\)
把向量縮成長度 1（正規化）之後，內積就等於 cos θ，只剩下方向。After normalising to length 1, the dot product equals cos θ: only direction remains.

word2vec：從文本中自動學出向量word2vec: learning the vectors from text

「觀其伴，知其義。」— Firth“You shall know a word by the company it keeps.” Mikolov et al. (2013): predict a word’s neighbours.
不需要任何人標註意義：模型為了預測周圍的詞，自己調整每個詞的向量。No one labels any meanings: to predict neighbours well, the model adjusts every word’s vector on its own.

word2vec ①：用內積打分Step 1: score a pair with the dot product

每個詞先有一個隨機向量。一對詞的內積 ＝ 分數：越高，越像鄰居。Every word starts with a random vector; the dot product of a pair is its score: higher = more neighbour-like.
真正的鄰居 real neighbour：思 · 學
隨機抽的詞 random word：思 · 香蕉
一開始分數是錯的：隨機詞「香蕉」反而比真鄰居「學」高。訓練目標：鄰居分數變高，隨機詞分數變低。At first the vectors are random, so the scores are wrong: the random word outscores the true neighbour. Training goal: raise neighbour scores, lower random-word scores.

word2vec ②：更新 ＝ 向量加法Step 2: the update is just vector addition

鄰居：把「學」的一小部分加到「思」上
\(\mathbf{思}\leftarrow\mathbf{思}+\eta\,\mathbf{學}\)
隨機詞：把「香蕉」的一小部分減掉
\(\mathbf{思}\leftarrow\mathbf{思}-\eta\,\mathbf{香蕉}\)
η（學習率）＝ 只加「一小部分」，例如 0.5 倍。中心詞和語境詞互相靠近：「學」也加上一小部分「思」（\(\mathbf{學}\leftarrow\mathbf{學}+\eta\,\mathbf{思}\)）。分數錯得越多，加得越多。η (learning rate): add only a fraction. Target and context move towards each other — 學 also gets a bit of 思. The more wrong the score, the larger the step.

總結：詞義可以用數字表示嗎？Summary: can word meaning be represented by numbers?

1
意義來自使用與語境（Saussure、Wittgenstein、Firth、Harris）。Meaning comes from use and context.
2
把詞表示成向量；用內積與餘弦相似度比較。Represent words as vectors; compare with dot product and cosine similarity.
3
稀疏的共現向量 → 稠密的詞向量（word2vec）。From sparse co-occurrence vectors to dense word vectors.
4
靜態詞向量：每個詞一個向量，無法區分一詞多義（下週：BERT）。Static vectors: one vector per word, so polysemy is blurred (next week: BERT).
向量是探索文本的工具，不是取代閱讀的答案機器：兩個詞的向量很接近時，仍要回到文本問「為什麼」。Vectors are a tool for exploring texts, not an answer machine: when two words are close, go back to the texts and ask why.
