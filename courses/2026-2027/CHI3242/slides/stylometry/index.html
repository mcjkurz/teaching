平均 μ 與標準差 σMean and standard deviation: where a word sits, and how much it varies between documents

五個詞，各在 30 篇文件裡的頻率：每個點是一篇文件five words, their frequency in 30 documents each; every dot is a document (illustrative)
平均 μ：這個詞通常用多少——每個詞都不一樣the mean: how often the word is used on average; different for every word
標準差 σ：文件之間差多少——「的」差很多，「呢」幾乎都一樣the standard deviation: how much documents differ; a lot for 的, hardly at all for 呢
同樣「差 2」：對「的」只是 0.3σ（很平常），對「呢」是 3.3σ（非常特別）→ 所以要除以 σThe same raw gap of 2 is ordinary for 的 but remarkable for 呢. That is why we divide by σ.

標準化：z 分數Standardization: the z-score

\(z = \dfrac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\)
μ（/mjuː/）：平均值；σ（/ˈsɪɡmə/）：標準差μ: the mean over all documents; σ: the standard deviation, i.e. the typical spread
