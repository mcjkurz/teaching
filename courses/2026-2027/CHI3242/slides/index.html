What is a collocation?

A combination of two words that show a tendency to occur near each other in natural language, i.e. to cooccur.
Evert, “Corpora and collocations”, p. 1214
Some words are attracted to each other:
cow – milkday – nightring – bell 喝 – 茶天氣 – 好
Others are not:
know – glassdoor – year喝 – 桌子
Question: how do we measure “attraction” — and tell it apart from chance?

Random words vs. real language

Null hypothesis (虛無假說): words are distributed randomly.
Natural language: words are distributed meaningfully.
A collocation is a pair that sits together more often than random shuffling would predict.

Observed frequency 觀察值 O

How often does word Y appear near target word X?
X target word: 王婆
window 3 words to the left and right of every 王婆 (horizon = 3)
Y collocate: 痛苦 — appears in a window 2 times.
O(王婆, 痛苦) = 2

Expected frequency 期望值 E

What would we see if everything were just chance?
10 balls, 5 red:  P(red) = 5 / 10 = 0.5
Draw 4 balls. How many are red? (this draw: 2)
E(red | 4) = P(red) × 4 = 0.5 × 4 = 2
One draw may give 1 or 3 — E is the average. Press R to draw again.

Expected frequency for words

Corpus: N = 1,000,000 tokens · count(the) = 100,000 · count(Iliad) = 10 · window = 5 left + 5 right
100 slots around the 10 “Iliad”s = 10 × 5 × 2
P(the) = 100,000 / 1,000,000 = 0.1
each slot is “the” with probability 0.1
So about 10% of the slots should be “the”:
E(the | Iliad) = P(the) × (count(Iliad) × window × 2)
= 0.1 × (10 × 5 × 2) = 10

A problem with simple measures

the Iliad

count(the) = 100,000
count(Iliad) = 10

E = 0.1 × (10 × 5 × 2) = 10

O = 10

must also

count(must) = 1,000
count(also) = 1,000

E = 0.001 × (1000 × 5 × 2) = 10

O = 10

Same O, same E — identical scores. But “Iliad” is rare and always accompanied by “the”; “must” and “also” are both frequent.
O vs. E ignores everything else in the corpus. We need to look at the cases where the words do not cooccur.

Three ways to define “near”

Same contingency table — but what do we count?

Window

滑動窗口 · surface

Y is within k words of X.

天氣那麼好我們

Count: tokens

Sentence

句子 · textual

X and Y are in the same sentence.

好天氣…散步

Count: sentences

Grammatical

語法關係 · syntactic

X and Y are in a syntactic relation (e.g. adjective → noun).

好→天氣

Count: grammatical pairs
