# 心靈生活 · CHI 4335 第 4 週

Open `index.html` in a browser. Keys: ← → step, 1–6 sections, G go to slide, L laser pointer, F fullscreen.

To play the two YouTube videos (Milgram experiment; Mischa Elman playing Schumann's Träumerei) inside the slides, serve the folder instead of opening the file directly:

    cd courses/2026-2027/CHI4335/slides/week-04-life-of-the-mind
    python3 -m http.server 8000

then open http://localhost:8000. (YouTube blocks embeds on pages opened as `file://`; there the slide shows a thumbnail that opens the video on YouTube.)

Reading: Tzvetan Todorov, "The Life of the Mind," in *Facing the Extreme: Moral Life in the Concentration Camps* (1996), pp. 91–118; the opening question is from p. 31. Tutorial: 高爾泰《尋找家園》(excerpts).

Sections: 集中營裏的日落 (the sunset passage, Todorov's question) · 英雄美德與普通美德 · 心靈生活 (Borowski, Levi, Ginzburg, Shalamov, Améry) · 智識與道德 (Mandl and Speer, Todorov on culture and moral acts, desk murderers, Milgram) · 記憶與遺忘 (Popiełuszko, the Warsaw Jewish Cemetery, the two Warsaw uprisings) · 同情的道德 (Träumerei, kindness and goodness, Lu Xun's slide, Todorov on reproach).
