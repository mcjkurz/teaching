# 創傷與敘事 · CHI 4335 第 6 週

Open `index.html` in a browser. Keys: ← → step, 1–6 sections, G go to slide, L laser pointer, F fullscreen.

To play the YouTube video (Krystian Zimerman playing Chopin's Ballade No. 1) inside the slide, serve the folder instead of opening the file directly:

    cd courses/2026-2027/CHI4335/slides/week-06-trauma-and-narrative
    python3 -m http.server 8000

then open http://localhost:8000. (YouTube blocks embeds on pages opened as `file://`; there the slide shows a thumbnail that opens the video on YouTube.)

Reading: Wendy O'Brien, "Telling Time: Literature, Temporality and Trauma". Passages from Leona Toker, *Gulag Literature and the Literature of Nazi Camps* (2019), ch. 4 "Forced Labor". Tutorial: 張翎《餘震》.

Sections: 勞動與重複 (Toker on unrepresentable labor; Shalamov's "Wheelbarrow" and Borowski's "This Way for the Gas" on repetition, mechanization and the production line) · 虛構與見證 (fictionality and its signposts, between fiction and non-fiction, irony, the language of the rulers and of the perpetrators) · 創傷與時間 (Terr, Husserl's time diagram, Freud, Caruth on belatedness, Nachträglichkeit, O'Brien on telling time and theodicy, Chopin's Ballade) · 陌生化 (Shklovsky, Tolstoy's "Kholstomer", Philomela) · 創傷理論 (Pederson's three modes, Craps and the second wave, PTSD in DSM-5 and ICD-11) · 文化創傷 (陶東風's lecture series, carrier group, audience, situation).
