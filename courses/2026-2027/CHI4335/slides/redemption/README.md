## The constellation of essays

The slide 「我們的星叢」 embeds `../../constellation/index.html`, a standalone page (it also works on its own, e.g. to show people later). It draws the 41 comparative essays of the semester. Every essay is an arc between the texts it compares. 念珠 shows the texts in syllabus order. 星叢 turns them into a rotating 3D constellation, with concepts used in three or more essays shown as blue stars. 導覽 cycles through quotes from the essays, and ‹ ❚❚ › (shown in the 星叢 view) step and pause. Click a text, concept or arc for the essays and quotes. URL hashes for testing: `#star`, `#tour`, `#instant`.

`constellation/data.js` is anonymized: for each essay it keeps only the texts compared, its key concepts and verbatim quotes (no names, student numbers, essay titles or summaries). The submissions it was built from are in `misc/towards-redemption/submissions/` (they contain names, so do not publish that folder).
